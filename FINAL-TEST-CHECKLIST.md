# Rent Master final UAT checklist

## Account and identity

- Register one Owner and one Tenant using new email addresses.
- Sign in and sign out from each account.
- Submit Aadhaar from one account and Passport from the other.
- Confirm the user sees the complete submitted number.
- Confirm the Admin sees the complete number and submitted file.
- Approve both users.
- Open Verified users and confirm both users appear with full details.
- Confirm an Owner or Tenant cannot open another user's identity file.

## Property photos and listing

- Create a property without selecting Publish and attach at least one photo.
- Confirm JPG, PNG and WEBP are accepted.
- Confirm an invalid file and a file over 8 MB are rejected.
- Confirm no more than 10 photos can be selected.
- Edit the property, remove and add photos.
- Publish the property and confirm the first photo appears in tenant search.
- Confirm a property cannot be published with no photos.
- Confirm incomplete old listings do not appear in tenant search.

## Rental journey

- Tenant opens the property, starts a conversation and submits an application.
- Owner opens the application and sends messages.
- Owner shortlists and accepts the application.
- Tenant confirms the tenancy.
- Test future move-in, actual handover, move-out request, approval and final handover.
- Confirm property status changes correctly throughout the journey.

## Ratings

- Complete the tenancy.
- Submit one Owner review and one Tenant review.
- Confirm the reviews wait for Admin review.
- Publish one review and reject the other with a reason.
- Confirm the published rating, stars, distribution and category averages are correct.
- Report the published review and confirm it disappears from the public rating until resolved.

## Help and support

- Submit a request as Owner and Tenant.
- Confirm each user sees only their own requests.
- Reply as Admin and mark the request In progress.
- Resolve it and confirm the user sees the reply and final status.

## Layout

- Check login, registration, property form, messages, reputation, Admin identity review and support at desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
- Confirm no visible text contains internal development wording.
